Using the Interactive Map

  • Colour by: Switch between Delivery category, Infrastructure type, Lead developer, and Alignment status in the sidebar legend, or click only beside any row to isolate a category.
  • Reference layers: Toggle the HS2 railway alignment, junction / intervention points, National Cycle Network, OS OpenRoads (10 km), and study area buffer.
  • Inspect & share: Click any route segment or point for full attributes and external street-level links, or copy the page URL to share the exact map view and active filters.
